        🌊 MarineMine Pro: Architecture & Algorithms

        Research Platform
        Project Objective

        MarineMine Pro is an advanced environmental data mining suite built to identify hidden patterns, trends, and risk hotspots in complex marine pollution datasets. It combines unsupervised clustering, supervised classification, association rule mining, and spatial GIS visualization to empower oceanographers and environmental scientists.

        Key Mining & ML Capabilities
        • K-Means Clustering: Identifies distinct pollution profiles using standardized multi-parameter vectors and PCA 2D projections.
        • Supervised Classifiers: Benchmarks Decision Trees, Random Forests, and Logistic Regression with confusion matrix telemetry.
        • Apriori Association Mining: Employs MLxtend to unearth co-occurrence patterns across environmental conditions.
        • Hotspot Spatial Analytics: Geo-aggregates coordinate observations via Leaflet.js to detect risk clusters.
        • Continuous Regression: Predicts continuous pollutant values with interactive What-If scenario simulations.
        Technology Stack
        Python 3.11 FastAPI SQLite & SQLAlchemy Scikit-learn MLxtend Pandas & NumPy Bootstrap 5 Chart.js Leaflet.js
        System Integrity

        Every metric, chart, and model in MarineMine Pro is calculated dynamically from the active user-uploaded or benchmark dataset. Zero hardcoded metrics.
